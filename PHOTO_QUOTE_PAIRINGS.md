---

## 1. PAIRING PRINCIPLES
1. **Visual Truth**: Quotes complement what is visibly happening in the frame. No dramatic love declarations placed next to casual landscape scenery.
2. **Respect for the Subject**: Nasreen's portraits receive affectionate, admiring, and dignified words—never objectifying or generic.
3. **Face Safe-Zones**: Text placement is strictly anchored (`beside`, `below`, or `overlay-safe-zone`) so text never touches or obscures a face.
4. **Single Source of Truth**: All UI components import from `content/photo-quote-pairings.ts`. No hardcoded couplings exist in JSX.
