# K & N — SCENE-BY-SCENE STORYBOARD
*A Continuous Nine-Scene Romantic Film in the Browser*

---

## SCENE 1: OVERTURE (THE HERO)
- **Atmosphere & Lighting**: Dawn blush (`#FFF9F6` transitioning to `#F8E6EA`), soft volumetric morning glow, warm champagne caustic sparkles.
- **3D World**: A silky rose satin ribbon floats in slow air; individual rose petals gently tumble from top to bottom; at the centre, an abstract refractive glass heart sculpture catches early morning light.
- **Camera / Scroll**: Slow 2.5-second push-in toward the sculpture. Mouse / gyro gently wobbles camera parallax by ±1.5 degrees.
- **Typography Sequence**:
  1. `K · A · U · S · E · E · N` emerges through masked letter-by-letter vertical reveals.
  2. `23 November 2020 — Forever` gracefully fades in beneath.
  3. `Six Years. One Beautiful Story.` draws on with a rose-gold hairline accent.
- **Actions / CTAs**:
  - Primary: `"Enter Our Story"` (scrolls smoothly into Scene 2).
  - Secondary: Floating scroll cue `"Scroll to discover"` with animated thread.
  - Quick skip: Discreet button `"Skip to Letters & Timeline"` for immediate direct access.
- **Audio Mood**: Delicate ambient piano overture with vinyl crackle.
- **Mobile Variant**: Single vertical column, tight display type scale, touch-scroll prompt, simplified single ribbon.

## SCENE 2: THE RIBBON BECOMES INFINITY
- **Visual Composition**: The visual metaphor of two separate lives converging. Two distinct ribbons (one warm rose, one champagne gold) emerge from opposite screen boundaries.
- **Scroll Behavior**: Scrubbed pinned timeline. As the user scrolls through the 150vh pinning zone, the two ribbons weave around each other, converge at the center, and lock into an infinity knot (`∞` lemniscate).
- **Center Reveal**: As the infinity knot completes, the K & N monogram (`K & N`) glows with a soft golden halo at the nexus.
- **Color State**: Shifts from `#F8E6EA` to `#E8BBC7` (dusty rose atmosphere).
- **Mobile Variant**: Vertical intertwine that gracefully fills portrait viewport without horizontal clipping.

## SCENE 4: THE GALLERY OF US (5 DISTINCT COMPOSITIONS)
- **Concept**: Anti-card grid. Five distinct, museum-grade layout formats:
  1. **Asymmetric Editorial Collage**: High-fashion magazine spread with overlapping captions and focal-point crops.
  2. **Cinematic Full-Bleed Frame**: Wide widescreen format with subtle scroll-linked zoom and golden dust overlay.
  3. **Vertical Portrait Stories**: Two tall arch-masked portraits celebrating individual and shared smiles.
  4. **Horizontal 35mm Film Strip**: Vintage film perforation border with drag-to-scroll interactivity.
  5. **Floating Polaroid Cluster**: Organic, slightly tilted polaroid frames with handwritten captions and gentle hover drift.
- **Lightbox**: Clicking any photograph opens a full-screen, high-res darkroom modal with zoom, caption, and keyboard arrows.

- **Visual Composition**: A luxury textured blush paper envelope seated in a softly lit burgundy alcove.

- **Interaction**:
  - Clicking `"Open Your Letter"` initiates a 3D envelope opening animation:

    3. Letter ascends smoothly onto the reading dais.

  - Letter Switcher: Tab between **Letter 01 — Six Years of Us** and **Letter 02 — For Everything Ahead**.
  - Printable / Save: One-click styled printable stationery version.
