## 1. Supported Devices & Browsers

The platform has been specifically tested and optimized for:
- **iPhone / iOS (Safari, Chrome):** FaceID / TouchID WebAuthn passkeys and Apple Passwords built-in authenticator.
- **Android (Chrome):** Biometric fingerprint passkeys and Google Authenticator.
- **Mac / Windows (Safari, Chrome, Edge):** TouchID, Windows Hello, and browser authenticators.

---

## 2. Recommended Enrolment (5 Minutes Together)

### Option A: Apple Passwords / Google Authenticator (Recommended & Simplest)
1. **Open the Login Page:** On her phone, open `https://k-and-n-gold.vercel.app`.
2. **Enter Credentials:**
   - Email: Her invited email.
   - Password: The memorable strong password you set for her.
3. **Step 2 (Security Factor):**
   - On your phone or laptop screen, show her the TOTP QR Code generated during provisioning.
   - She opens her Camera or Authenticator App and taps **Add Verification Code**.
   - She enters the 6-digit code.
4. **Welcome Screen:**
   - The screen gently blooms with:
     *“Identity Verified · Welcome to Our Little World.”*
   - Her session cookie is established for **7 days**. She will not have to re-enter anything during her celebration exploration.

### Option B: Biometric Passkey / Face ID
- After logging in, she can tap **"Register This Device with FaceID / TouchID"** under settings.
- Her phone's native prompt will ask: *"Save a Passkey for K & N Anniversary?"*
- She taps **Continue**. Next time, unlocking the site takes only a single glance or tap of her finger!
